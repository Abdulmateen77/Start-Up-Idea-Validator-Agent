# Workflow: Merge — Synthesize the One-Page Recommendation

## Objective
Turn the Skeptic's surviving claims into a single one-page recommendation the human
can read in under a minute.

## Required Inputs
- Surviving claims (+ caveats) and noted gaps from Skeptic.
- Original idea brief from Intake (niche, audience, core question), so the
  recommendation stays anchored to what was actually asked.

## Steps
1. Re-state the core question at the top.
2. Group surviving claims by lane, lead with the ones that most directly bear on the
   core question.
3. Write a short, plain verdict: what the evidence supports, what it doesn't, and
   where the real uncertainty is (the gaps Skeptic flagged).
4. Do not smooth over conflicting evidence or gaps to produce a falsely confident
   verdict — a recommendation of "not enough evidence yet, here's what's missing" is
   a legitimate, useful output.
5. Hand the finished one-pager to Human Gate.

## Tools
None yet. Pure synthesis over Skeptic's output — no external calls expected.

## Expected Output
A one-page recommendation with: core question, key findings (with caveats/sources
where relevant), stated gaps, and a plain verdict. No next-move menu here — that's
Human Gate's job.

## Edge Cases
- **Skeptic killed almost every claim**: say so directly; recommend what specific
  research would need to happen before a real decision is possible.
- **Evidence points different directions across lanes** (e.g. strong customer pain
  but crowded competition): surface the tension explicitly rather than averaging it
  into a mushy middle verdict.
